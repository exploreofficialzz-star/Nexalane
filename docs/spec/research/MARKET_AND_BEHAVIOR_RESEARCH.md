# CURRENT MARKET + PLAYER BEHAVIOR RESEARCH

## 1. Subway Surfers as benchmark, not template
Current Google Play listings show Subway Surfers remains a large, continuously updated free-to-play runner with ads and in-app purchases. The lesson is the durability of the genre's simple controls, collection, progression, recurring content and monetization — not the need to duplicate its creative expression.

## 2. Current retention benchmark
GameAnalytics' August 24, 2026 mobile benchmark report says that in its 2025 global dataset the median mobile game was around 22% D1 retention, just under 4% D7 and about 0.7–0.8% D30; the top 1% were dramatically higher. The report also says median games averaged roughly 3.1–3.5 minute sessions and about 3.8 sessions/day, while engagement was much stronger among top performers.

Implication: optimize the first minutes and build layered short-horizon, daily and long-horizon loops rather than requiring long single sessions.

## 3. Hybrid-casual shift
Recent 2025 casual/hybrid-casual reporting from AppMagic describes stronger monetization and retention systems around deeper mechanics, events and repeatable competitive structures.

Implication: NEXALANE should remain instantly accessible like an arcade runner while having enough progression, events and collection to support durable engagement.

## 4. Live-ops evidence
Current casual-game live-ops examples increasingly connect events directly to the core mechanic instead of making the event feel like a completely separate minigame.

Implication: seasonal events should alter the runner, routes, modifiers, goals or rewards while preserving the base skill loop.

## 5. Mobile technical reality
Godot's 4.7 documentation supports Android export, and the current renderer guidance makes Mobile/Compatibility more appropriate for mobile targets than using a desktop-first Forward+ strategy.

Implication: performance architecture is a first-class product feature.

## 6. Monetization
The project should use hybrid monetization: voluntary rewarded ads plus IAP. Ads provide broad monetization coverage; IAP creates a higher-value path for engaged players.

Implication: monetization should be measured alongside retention, not against it.
