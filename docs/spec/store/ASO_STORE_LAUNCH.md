## Short description draft
Run through a living 3D city, choose your route, chain perfect moves, and outrun the Gridwatch.

## Long description framework
OPENING HOOK
A cinematic 3D endless runner built around speed, route choice and skill.

FEATURE BLOCKS
- Run through 8 districts.
- Choose street, transit, rooftop and hidden routes.
- Build FLOW through precision movement.
- Unlock original runners and cosmetics.
- Compete in daily and weekly challenges.
- Race your ghost and chase your best run.
- Join seasonal events with fresh rewards.

MONETIZATION TRANSPARENCY
Free to play. Optional rewarded ads and optional in-app purchases.
