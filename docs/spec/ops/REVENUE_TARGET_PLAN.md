# $50K REVENUE TARGET PLAN

## Target
**$50,000 gross revenue by December 31, 2026.**

As of October 1, 2026, this is an aggressive target. The plan must therefore use staged risk control rather than assuming paid UA will immediately scale.

## Core business equation
Revenue = active users × monetization per user × time.

Scalable acquisition equation:
LTV > CAC.

## Illustrative arithmetic
These are examples only:
- 100,000 installs × $0.50 gross revenue/install = $50,000 gross.
- 250,000 installs × $0.20 = $50,000 gross.
- 500,000 installs × $0.10 = $50,000 gross.
- 1,000,000 installs × $0.05 = $50,000 gross.

The project should NOT chase downloads at the expense of retention.

## Daily run-rate perspective
If revenue were evenly distributed over the remaining 92 days from Oct 1 through Dec 31, the target would average about $543/day. Actual revenue will not be even, so the business plan should use milestones.

## Recommended milestones
### Milestone A — Product health
- stable first session;
- strong onboarding completion;
- D1/D7 cohorts measured;
- stable device performance.

### Milestone B — Monetization health
- rewarded ads functional;
- starter offer functional;
- purchase completion validated;
- no major retention damage from monetization.

### Milestone C — Soft-launch economics
Calculate:
- CPI;
- D1/D7/D14 retention;
- ARPDAU;
- modeled LTV;
- payback window.

### Milestone D — Scale
Only increase paid acquisition when:
1. cohorts retain;
2. store conversion is acceptable;
3. ad/IAP monetization is stable;
4. LTV can plausibly exceed CAC.

## Acquisition channels
Test creative across:
- TikTok-style vertical video;
- Meta placements;
- YouTube Shorts-style creative;
- organic gameplay clips;
- store listing experiments.

## Creative testing
Produce multiple hooks, not one trailer.

Measure each creative by:
- thumb-stop/view rate where available;
- click-through;
- install rate;
- D1/D7 retention of acquired users;
- revenue by cohort.

## Revenue protection
Do not chase $50k through aggressive ad frequency if the retention loss lowers total LTV.

## Reporting
Weekly:
- gross revenue;
- ad revenue;
- IAP revenue;
- DAU;
- installs;
- retention;
- ARPDAU;
- CPI;
- modeled LTV;
- spend;
- ROAS where enough data exists.
